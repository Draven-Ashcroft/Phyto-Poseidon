Positive hydrotropism: roots curve toward regions of higher soil moisture, bending growth away from the straight vertical path even though gravity is acting equally on both pots. The magnifier's auxin explanation is a simplified teaching analogy borrowed from gravitropism's mechanism, not the currently documented driver of hydrotropism (research points to cytokinin/MIZ1 signaling instead) — and it isn't part of the NCERT syllabus, which describes hydrotropism observationally only.
